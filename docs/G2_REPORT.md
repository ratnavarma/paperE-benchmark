Date: October 20, 2026 (target October 31). Pilot on development seeds 61000–61019 (20 seeds), all 22 F0-R cells, frozen configurations from results/TUNING_FROZEN.json. Every number is development. Source: results/g2_numbers.json, regenerated by `uv run python -m papere.analysis --g2 results/f0r_dev --frozen results/TUNING_FROZEN.json`. Units: kWh per 100 participants per event. Intervals: paired bootstrap over seeds, 10,000 resamples, 95%.

## What failed, and what the gate says

- **The candidate's per-participant advantage was not detected.** E1 at the G2 stratum (M = 3, ρ = 0.95) is **−0.375, interval [−1.25, +0.50]**, "not detected at this size". The point estimate is negative: the candidate's lead over the best oblivious policy is smaller at δ_A ∈ {2, 4} than at δ_A ∈ {0, 1}.
- **A population-rate rule reaches the 80% bar.** The population-rate scenario MPC's gain over the tuned symmetric margin is 0.95 of the candidate's gain (interval [0.63, 1.38]). By PLAN Section 8 that is the headline: at this cell, most of the gain over margin rules comes from planning with any stochastic model of unconfirmed delivery, not from per-participant history.
- **The in-progress format is not forced, by a tie.** The rule forces the 3–4 page format if the E1 interval sits wholly below 0.5. Its upper end is 0.50 exactly, at the data's granularity, so by the strict rule it does not sit wholly below. This is the narrowest possible miss and should be read as borderline, not as evidence for the full format. The format is the owner's decision (PLAN Section 11, item 5).
- Persistence does not reach the bar: its gain over the margin is −0.17 [−0.38, 0.00] (ratio −0.05).

The oracle, which knows each participant's true class, beats the best oblivious policy at δ_A = 2 by 1.13 with an interval above zero. Per-participant information therefore has value in this cell; e1, learning it from three prior events, recovers about 0.17 of it here. The oracle-share ratio of the E1 contrasts (−0.51) is not interpretable because the candidate's contrast is negative.

## Context and caveats

- 20 seeds only. Every interval above is wide; the full run on 61000–61099 follows the run order and will replace these values.
- On the tuning seeds, the frozen objectives put the candidate ahead of the population-rate MPC by 1.0, 1.3 and 1.8 at δ_A = 1, 2 and 4. Those values are in-sample (each family's configuration was selected on the same seeds), so they overstate; the pilot is out of sample.
- During the build, unit tests ran some policies on seeds in 61000–61099 (docs/SEED_LEDGER.md, disclosure). No tuning choice was made on them.
- Negative controls: NC2 and NC4 pass; NC1 and NC1_sp stay FAIL as pre-declared, with NC1-exact passing for the planning policies; NC3′ fails at 50 and at 250 seeds on interval width in one cell, with one small detected difference against e1 inside the band (docs/NC_REPORT.md).

## What this means for the note

The pre-declared reading is that the headline becomes "a population-rate stochastic MPC copies the gain over margin rules", with the per-participant advantage of e1 not detected at the pilot size. The full run decides whether that holds at 100 seeds. The owner decides the page format.
